Selected work

Smart Traffic Detection System

In progress

Watches road footage and reports what's on it: which vehicles are present, how they move, and how congested the road is.

I fine-tuned YOLOv8 on BDD100K dashcam images and test it on UA-DETRAC, a dataset it never trained on. It tracks vehicles across frames, classifies traffic level, and serves results through a FastAPI app.

Obesity Level Classification

Completed

An end-to-end ML pipeline that predicts one of seven obesity levels from a person's eating habits and physical condition, using the NObeyesdad dataset.

I compared Logistic Regression, Decision Tree, KNN and Random Forest, tuned the Random Forest with GridSearchCV, and wrote it up as an IEEE-style paper.

  • scikit-learn
  • pandas
